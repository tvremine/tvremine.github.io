Production

The show, held together.

Freelance live-event producing and production project management for conferences, receptions, and hybrid programs. Based in Indianapolis. Book it on its own, or with aerial coverage from the same person.

Services

What gets held.

  • 01

    Event producer

    Own the show from load-in through strike: the schedule, the stakeholders, and the calls that keep the room on time.

  • 02

    Technical & production project management

    Scope, timeline, and the technical plan in one place, so audio, video, lighting, and staging are not running separate shows.

  • 03

    AV & live production planning

    Room layout, signal flow, power, and a gear list matched to the brief — not pulled from a catalog.

  • 04

    Show calling support

    Call the show, or sit with the caller, so cues, walks, and holds land where the rundown says they do.

  • 05

    Vendor & labor coordination

    One contact for vendors, crew calls, and the handoffs between house staff and show labor.

  • 06

    Budgeting

    Build the production budget and track it from the first estimate through closeout.

  • 07

    Run-of-show

    A rundown the crew can follow: cues, times, owners, and the changes that show up on the day.

  • 08

    Hybrid & livestream production

    A room that works for the people in the seats and the people on the stream, planned as one show.

With the aircraft

One call sheet. Two jobs.

When an event needs the air and the ground, they are planned together. Flight path, call time, and rundown come from the same brief, so the aircraft is part of the show.

Aerial capture stays a Waypoint Aerial service. Production and project management can be booked without a flight.
