Privacy policy

Last updated September 13, 2026

Who we are

Waypoint Aerial is the Part 107 aerial studio of Tom ReMine, based in Indianapolis, Indiana. Questions about this policy: tvremine@gmail.com.

What we collect

The quote form asks for name, email, phone, preferred date, site or venue, service type, and a short brief. That is the information needed to check airspace and reply with a number.

We do not run analytics, advertising pixels, or a cookie banner on this site today. We do load typefaces from Google Fonts.

How it is used

Quote details stay in your browser (local storage) so you have a reference number, and are used to follow up if you email us. We do not sell or rent contact information. We do not use it for unrelated marketing lists.

Email

If you write tvremine@gmail.com, that message lives in ordinary email (currently Gmail) and is used to plan and fly the job. Keep operational details in email rather than in the public form if they are sensitive.

How long we keep it

Browser-stored requests last until you clear site data. Email about a job is kept as long as needed to fly, deliver, invoice, and meet record-keeping for commercial sUAS work.

Your requests

Email us to correct or delete a quote request we still have. We will handle it as soon as practical.
