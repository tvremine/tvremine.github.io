Terms of work

Last updated September 13, 2026

Studio

These terms cover commercial small unmanned aircraft work flown by Tom ReMine under Waypoint Aerial, an FAA Part 107 operation based in Indianapolis, Indiana. A written confirmation (email is enough) is the booking. Website ranges are typical, not a contract price.

Quotes

A quote is an estimate for the brief you sent. It becomes a job when both sides confirm date, site, deliverables, and fee. Travel outside Central Indiana, extra airframes, or night/waiver work may change the number.

Airspace, weather, and access

Flights follow FAA rules, LAANC or authorizations, TFRs, and venue policy. We can delay, shorten, or cancel a flight when weather, airspace, or site conditions make it unsafe or unlawful. We will reschedule in good faith. The client arranges site access, property-owner permission, and any venue COI naming requirements in time for the call.

Deliverables

Unless a job email says otherwise, you receive a license to use the commissioned stills, clips, or maps for the project you hired us for — including client and venue use. Waypoint Aerial keeps copyright and may show frames in a portfolio unless you ask us in writing not to (common for private residences and unreleased work). Raw sensor files are not included unless quoted.

Payment

Payment terms are in the confirmation email. Late or missing payment can pause delivery. Cancellations inside 48 hours of call time may be billed a portion of the fee if we cannot fill the slot; weather and TFR cancellations on our side are not billed.

Liability

Commercial sUAS liability insurance is carried for operations. Certificates of insurance are issued to venues on request. Our liability for a given job is limited to the fee paid for that job, except where the law does not allow that limit. We are not responsible for missed marketing windows, event delays, or third party claims that are not caused by our flight.

Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Indiana. They are the working terms of the studio, not a substitute for a negotiated production contract on larger jobs.
